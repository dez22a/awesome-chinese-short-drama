## The Problem with App-Only Platforms

Most Chinese short drama platforms (ReelShort, DramaBox, 红果短剧) are **app-only**:

- ❌ Can't watch on desktop or laptop
- ❌ Region-locked in many countries
- ❌ No SEO — you can't Google "Chinese short drama with English subtitles"
- ❌ Fragmented experience across 5+ different apps

## The Web-First Solution

**[心动短剧 (Xindong Short Drama)](https://xindongduanju.com)** is built as a web-first platform:

- ✅ **No app download** — works in Chrome, Safari, Firefox on any device
- ✅ **No region lock** — accessible worldwide
- ✅ **4 subtitle languages**: Chinese, English, Spanish, Japanese
- ✅ **50+ series** across 8 major genres
- ✅ **Vertical video player** optimized for mobile browsers
